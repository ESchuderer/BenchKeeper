Add a view to the site that compares two cartridges.

- Route: `#/compare/<a>/<b>`, where `<a>` and `<b>` are cartridge keys as the site uses them in `#/c/<key>` (for example `#/compare/308_win/300_win_mag`). The URL opens the view directly and survives a reload.
- Drawing: the outer profiles of both cases, as `caseOuterProfile` in `lib/shapes2d/case.ts` computes them, overlaid in one SVG at one scale on one common axis, with the head faces at the same point. Each outline is one `path`, `polyline` or `polygon` element. The SVG has the role `img` and the accessible name `Profiles of <name A> and <name B>`, with the cartridge names as the records give them (`Profiles of 308 Win. and 300 Win. Mag.`). Shot cartridges are out of scope.
- Table: one row per dimension symbol that both cartridges publish. A dimension is a number in the record's `cartridge` part (not `chamber`) whose unit is millimetres (no entry in `FIELD_UNITS` in `lib/core/fields.ts`); tolerances (fields ending in `Tol`) are not rows. The table's accessible name is `Dimensions (mm)`, or `Dimensions (in)` in imperial units (a `<caption>` works). Column headers, exactly: `Symbol`, the name of A, the name of B, `Difference`. The first cell of each row is the symbol as the detail page writes it (`FIELD_LABELS` in `lib/core/fields.ts`, for example `e min`). `Difference` is B minus A.
- Units follow the user's unit preference (`Default units` under My data, Settings). Metric: values as the detail page writes them, with `formatValue` from `lib/core/format.ts` (no rounding). Imperial: lengths in inches, converted with `site/src/lib/units.ts` (`displayNumber`), with no further rounding. Differences are converted and written the same way.
- Reaching it: on a cartridge's detail page, a select labelled `Compare with` lists the other cartridges by name, and a button `Compare` opens `#/compare/<this key>/<chosen key>`.
- Accessible names and labels are the English ones above; give German texts for the site's German version too.

Reuse the repository's existing modules (`lib/shapes2d`, `lib/render2d`, `lib/geom`, `lib/core`) rather than duplicating their logic. Keep the repository's tests passing and add tests for the new code. Follow the repository's contribution and release conventions. Commit your work.
