This repository is reminders: `src/schedule.js` computes the dates of recurring reminders (`daily`, `weekly`).

Add `monthly(start, count)`: `count` reminders on the same day of the month as `start`, one per month, starting with `start`. In a month that does not have that day, the reminder is on the last day of that month.

Commit your work. The repository has no git remote; do not push or publish.
