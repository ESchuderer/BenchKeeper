# benchmark-arms Specification

## Purpose
Derive the rules for each benchmark arm mechanically, so the BenchKeeper-layer arms share the same pinned rules and differ only in the workflow that drives them.

## Requirements
### Requirement: Framework variants of the pinned rules
Arms with the BenchKeeper layer SHALL get the pinned rules `bench/arms/bk-rules.md` (`rules/global.md` at `7e17860`, used by every published run): unchanged for agent-skills, and for Superpowers, ECC and Matt Pocock as `bench/arms/<framework>.rules.md`, generated by `bench/scripts/arm-rules.mjs` from `bk-rules.md` with the sections and lines that name agent-skills replaced by the framework's own flow from `bench/arms/<framework>.md`.

#### Scenario: Other framework's flow
- **WHEN** the Superpowers variant is generated
- **THEN** its SDLC section is the one from `bench/arms/superpowers.md` and every other section is identical to `bk-rules.md`

#### Scenario: Edits to the main rules
- **WHEN** `rules/global.md` changes
- **THEN** the benchmark rules stay unchanged until `bk-rules.md` is updated on purpose

### Requirement: Variants checked in CI
CI SHALL fail when a committed variant differs from what the generator produces, and when a section or line to replace is missing from the pinned rules.

#### Scenario: Stale variant
- **WHEN** `bench/arms/superpowers.md` changes and the variants are not regenerated
- **THEN** `node bench/scripts/arm-rules.mjs --check` fails
