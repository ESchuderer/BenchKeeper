## Owner questions

- A question the owner need not answer at once becomes an issue in the hub repository `{{hub}}`, never in a product repository: labels `question`, the project's `project:<name>` label and `ready-for-human`.
- The body gives What (the topic), Impact (per option) and Why now, then the options as checkboxes (`- [ ]`). The recommended option comes first, marked "(Recommended)", with its reason, source and confidence.
- Never wait in a session for the answer. Take the recommended default when the task is clear and the default is safe and reversible, and mark it "pending owner decision"; otherwise push, write a handoff on the pull request that names the issue, and stop.
- A question asked while the owner is at the terminal stays in the session.
